Quick Overview: What is Medical Bill Explainer?

  • Core Mission: To bridge the asymmetric knowledge gap between multi-billion-dollar hospital billing conglomerates and ordinary patients facing complex invoices.
  • Free Educational Tools: We provide an instant, client-side medical bill decoder, step-by-step Explanation of Benefits (EOB) calculators, 3 customizable dispute letters, and a database of 57 common CPT billing codes.
  • Privacy Architecture: Bill text entered into the Explainer Tool is processed locally in your browser and is not sent to this site's application server or stored in its database. Do not enter names, member IDs, claim numbers, or other sensitive information.
  • Editorial Standards: Code definitions and billing guidance are checked against official public sources where available. Price ranges are broad educational estimates, not quotes or guarantees.

Why This Project Exists

In the United States, receiving medical care is frequently followed by a second trauma: the arrival of an itemized hospital bill or a multi-page document labeled "THIS IS NOT A BILL" (your Explanation of Benefits).

Billing audits and consumer studies have reported high error rates in some samples, but results vary substantially by bill type, audit method, and definition of an error. We use this site to help patients identify questions to verify—not to declare that a particular bill is incorrect, unlawful, or fraudulent.

Patients are routinely expected to write checks for thousands of dollars without knowing what a "CPT 99284" represents, whether a facility fee was lawful under the No Surprises Act, or why their insurance company approved an "Allowed Amount" that vastly differs from the doctor's initial bill. Medical Bill Explainer was created to give everyday families the plain-English decoder ring they need to question, verify, and dispute their medical bills before parting with their hard-earned money.

Our Editorial Standards & Research Methodology

Trust in healthcare financial information requires careful use of primary sources. We prioritize official government guidance and identify uncertainty where rules vary by plan, provider, state, or date of service. Third-party summaries may be used for context, but they are not treated as a substitute for the governing source.

1. Official Regulatory Frameworks

We review federal consumer-protection and billing guidance, including the No Surprises Act, CMS resources, CFPB materials, and EMTALA information. Legal protections can depend on the service, plan, provider, state, and date of care.

2. CMS Datasets & NCCI Edits

Where relevant, we consult CMS National Correct Coding Initiative (NCCI) materials and Medicare Physician Fee Schedule (MPFS) resources. These references do not determine a user's commercial insurance payment or medical necessity.

3. AMA Coding Principles

CPT® (Current Procedural Terminology) codes are copyright of the American Medical Association (AMA). We provide strictly educational descriptions designed to help consumers recognize medical terminology without substituting formal medical necessity reviews.

4. Objective Fair Price Benchmarks

Our price brackets are broad educational estimates assembled from public pricing and reimbursement references. They are not quotes, negotiated insurance rates, or predictions of a specific patient's responsibility. Facility fees, professional fees, geography, network status, and plan rules can materially change the amount.

Privacy by Architecture: Zero Server-Side PHI

Most internet companies collect, store, and monetize user data. Because medical invoices frequently contain deeply private information—such as diagnostic tests, pathology exams, doctor visits, and personal financial figures—we made a foundational architectural decision:

Bill text entered into our Explainer Tool and values entered into the EOB calculator are processed locally in your browser. The bill text is not sent to this site's application server or stored in its database. The site may still load analytics and other third-party resources described in the Privacy Policy; do not enter names, member IDs, claim numbers, or other sensitive information.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can Medical Bill Explainer give me clinical medical advice?

No. Medical Bill Explainer is an educational publication dedicated exclusively to financial and billing transparency. We do not assess clinical treatments, diagnose conditions, or evaluate whether a medical procedure was clinically indicated. Always speak with your licensed healthcare provider regarding medical decisions.

Can your tool dispute my hospital bill on my behalf?

We are not a law firm, billing agency, or power-of-attorney service. We empower you with educational tools, dispute letter templates, and negotiation scripts so that you can directly contact your hospital billing department or insurance claims adjuster with confidence.

How accurate are the CPT code prices shown on the site?

Our price ranges reflect typical fair-market cash costs and regional in-network commercial allowances synthesized from public CMS fee schedules and hospital chargemaster disclosures. Actual healthcare charges vary widely based on your geographic location, facility type (e.g., hospital outpatient department vs. independent physician clinic), and individual insurance contract terms.
