How the Bill Decoder Protects You

🔍

1. CPT & HCPCS Code Recognition

Doctors bill insurance using 5-digit CPT procedure codes (e.g. 99214 for a complex visit or 71046 for an X-ray). Our tool detects these codes and matches them with plain English descriptions and fair cash benchmarks so you know what you are being billed for.

📑

2. Insurance Jargon Decoded

Terms like Allowed Amount, Coinsurance, and Facility Fees are designed to confuse patients. We translate every detected term so you know whether a charge is your legal responsibility or a contractual write-off.

⚠️

3. Duplicate Charge Detection

One of the most common hospital billing errors is duplicate billing (e.g. charging twice for the same blood draw or medication). Our scanner checks code frequencies and alerts you if a duplicate appears.

⚖️

4. Balance Billing & EOB Comparison

Never pay a medical bill before comparing it with your insurance Explanation of Benefits. If the provider bills you more than the EOB says you owe, you can use our EOB Discrepancy Calculator to generate an immediate dispute script.

Frequently Asked Questions

What if my bill doesn't have 5-digit CPT codes?

Hospitals frequently send "summary bills" that just say general words like "Lab: $800" or "Pharmacy: $1,200". By law, you have the right to request a detailed, itemized statement. Call the billing office and say: "Please send me an itemized statement with all CPT and HCPCS procedure codes."

Can I paste a PDF or image into this tool?

If your bill is a PDF, simply open it on your computer or phone, select/highlight the text, and copy-paste it into the box. If you have a paper bill, you can copy the text using your smartphone camera's "Live Text" feature.

How does this tool help me lower my medical bill?

Knowledge is your strongest negotiating lever. Once you see the fair cash price range for your codes, you can call the hospital billing department and ask for a prompt-pay cash discount, question charges that exceed national benchmarks, or apply for hospital charity care.
